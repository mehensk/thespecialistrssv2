Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and the property buying process

Do you assist with financing?

Yes, we work with partner banks and financial institutions to help coordinate mortgage applications and requirements. We can guide you through the entire financing process.

How do I schedule a property viewing?

Simply use our contact form or call us directly. We typically confirm viewing appointments within one business day and can arrange visits at your convenience.

Do you handle rental properties?

Yes, we manage select rental properties in Metro Manila and Cebu. Contact us to see available rental listings and discuss your requirements.

Can you help me list my property?

Absolutely. We'll review your property details, provide a market analysis, and propose competitive pricing and marketing strategies to maximize your sale potential.

What areas do you cover?

We specialize in Metro Manila (Makati, BGC, Quezon City, Ortigas) and Cebu, with select properties in other key markets. Contact us to discuss your specific area of interest.

What are your service fees?

Our fees are competitive and transparent. We'll discuss all costs upfront during our initial consultation. Typically, buyer representation is free, and seller fees are commission-based.
